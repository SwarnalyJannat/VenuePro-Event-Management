Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 6, 2026 · Effective for All Customer Reservations

1. Commitment to Privacy & Platform Security

VenuePro Enterprise Event Management ("VenuePro", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and data integrity of all users, including event organizers, guests, partner caterers, staffing personnel, and enterprise administrators.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your personal and enterprise information across our web applications, booking engines, and logistics services.

2. Information We Collect from Customers

We collect information necessary to coordinate events, process transactions, and guarantee safety:

  • Client & Organizer Details: Name, corporate affiliation, contact phone, billing address, and government identification where required.
  • Booking & Event Parameters: Event dates, venue reservations, expected guest headcount, floor plans, and staging specifications.
  • Culinary & Dietary Specifications: Selected catering tiers, allergen restrictions, menu adjustments, and guest count revisions.
  • Vendor & Staff Credentials: Commercial kitchen permits, National ID (NID), curriculum vitae (CV), and security clearances for on-site staff.
  • Transactional & Payment Data: Invoice histories, payment method confirmations (tokenized via SSL/PCI-DSS certified processors), and settlement records.

3. How Information Is Utilized

Your data is processed strictly for operational and governance purposes:

  • Orchestrating venue reservations and verifying calendar availability without date conflicts.
  • Transmitting real-time order specifications to verified commercial catering partner kitchens.
  • Coordinating event setup checklists and staffing deployments for on-site event managers.
  • Issuing formal corporate tax invoices, receipts, and downloadable accounting statements.
  • Maintaining audit trails compliant with SOC-2 enterprise data governance standards.

4. Data Protection & Encryption Standards

All sensitive information transmitted through VenuePro is encrypted in transit using 256-bit TLS/SSL protocols. Server data storage incorporates AES-256 encryption at rest, role-based access controls (RBAC), and automated threat detection.

5. Information Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

VenuePro does not sell, lease, or monetize user data. Information is only disclosed to authorized parties directly involved in executing your reservation (e.g., the assigned caterer receiving meal count breakdowns or on-site security checking guest registries).

6. Your Rights & Data Management

You retain the right to inspect, correct, or request the archival of your personal records. For compliance inquiries or data access requests, please contact our Data Protection Office via the Contact Support portal.
