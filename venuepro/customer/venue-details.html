About the Venue

The Grand Emerald Ballroom stands as the pinnacle of our enterprise venue collection. Spanning over 8,500 square feet of unobstructed space, it features a unique blend of neo-classical architecture and modern technological integration. Designed to host up to 600 guests, the ballroom offers state-of-the-art acoustics, a dedicated VIP lounge, and private access elevators for high-profile attendees.

Whether you are hosting a multinational corporate summit, a high-fashion runway event, or a prestigious gala, the ballroom's modular layout and sophisticated aesthetic provide the perfect canvas for your vision.
